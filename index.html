How to play

Meet people, tick squares, win a prize!

  1. Find someone who matches a square and have a quick chat.
  2. Tap the square to tick it off. Tap again if you made a mistake.
  3. One person per square, so you'll meet 16 different people.
  4. Tick all 16, add your name, and you're in tonight's prize draw.
